# Privacy Policy — Blade Hunter: Idle Slayer

_Last updated: replace this date when you actually publish._

Blade Hunter: Idle Slayer ("the App") does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data from its users.

## Data collection
- All game progress (Essence, generators owned, achievements, prestige state, boost timers) is stored **only locally on your device** using standard web storage (`localStorage`), inside the app's own sandboxed storage area.
- The App does not require an account, login, or any personally identifiable information.
- The App makes no network requests of its own — it does not send gameplay data anywhere.

## Advertising
The App shows optional, user-initiated rewarded video ads (via Google AdMob) that the player chooses to watch in exchange for an in-game boost. Google AdMob may collect data (such as advertising ID and IP address) as described in Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. This collection is performed by the AdMob SDK, not by the App directly, and only occurs when the player actively taps to watch an ad.

## Children's privacy
This game is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children. If ads are configured to serve to a general audience, refer to Google AdMob's policies on child-directed treatment when configuring ad settings in the Play Console.

## Changes to this policy
This policy may be updated if the App's data practices change. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
